What this app is

Fvgazi_90 is a personal tool run by the owner of the Fvgazi football page. It turns football news into photo carousels and, when the owner chooses, sends them to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Use of TikTok

The app only acts on the TikTok account that has logged in and given permission. It sends content to that account's drafts or inbox. The account owner reviews, edits and publishes everything themselves in the TikTok app. Use of TikTok is also covered by TikTok's own Terms of Service.

Content

Posts are football commentary and banter based on public news reports, with links to the original sources. The page owner is responsible for what gets published.
