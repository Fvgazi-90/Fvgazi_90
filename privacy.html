Fvgazi_90 - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Who uses this app

Fvgazi_90 is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Fvgazi football page. It is not offered to the public.

What data it uses

  • TikTok login: when the owner connects their TikTok account, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (such as display name). This is used only to send the owner's own carousels to their TikTok drafts.
  • Images: carousel images are hosted publicly on this website so TikTok can fetch them.

Where it is stored

Login tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sold, shared or sent anywhere except to TikTok when the app makes a request.

What it does not do

The app does not collect data about other TikTok users or viewers, use tracking cookies, or show ads.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services permissions. Deleting the app's data folder removes the stored tokens.
